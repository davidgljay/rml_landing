Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Relationality Lab ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and our events, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

  • Information you give us. When you fill out a form on this site (contact, newsletter, or event signup), we collect the details you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number, and any message you send.
  • Survey responses. If you opt in to be surveyed after an event, we collect your responses to those surveys.
  • Automatically collected information. We use privacy-focused analytics (Umami) to understand how the site is used. This may include your approximate location, browser and device type, pages viewed, and referring site. It is not used to identify you personally.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your messages and requests.
  • To register you for events and send logistical updates about them.
  • To send our newsletter and event announcements, if you have subscribed.
  • To send post-event survey text messages, only if you have expressly consented.
  • To conduct research on relationships and community, and to improve our events and website. Research findings are reported in aggregate and do not identify you unless you give separate permission.
  • To maintain the security of the site and comply with legal obligations.

3. Text Messages (SMS)

We send text messages only if you opt in through a form on this site. Consent to receive text messages is never a condition of registering for an event or of any purchase. Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to cancel, or HELP for assistance. We do not sell or share your phone number or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us operate, such as email delivery, form handling, automation, survey and text-messaging platforms, and analytics. They may process your information only on our behalf and for those purposes. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our community.

5. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, or as required by law. Contact and event registration details are generally kept for up to three years after our last interaction with you. Aggregated or de-identified research data may be kept longer.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Email: You can unsubscribe from any email using the link at the bottom of the message.
  • Text messages: Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Access, correction, deletion: You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Depending on where you live (for example, California, or the European Economic Area and UK), you may have additional legal rights, including to object to or restrict processing and to data portability.

To make a request, use the contact form. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Our site and events are intended for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Third-Party Links

This site links to third-party services (such as Substack and our book site). Their privacy practices are their own, and we encourage you to review them.

10. International Users

We are based in the United States, and your information may be processed there. By using this site, you understand that your information may be transferred to and stored in the United States.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed. Material changes will be posted on this page.

12. Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the contact form.
